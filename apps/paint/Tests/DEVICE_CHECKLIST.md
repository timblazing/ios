# Paint device checklist

Run on the iPad mini 2 after installing a new build.

- [ ] App icon shows the paint cup on the blue sky and green hill.
- [ ] Landscape and portrait both lay out with nothing clipped; rotating mid-stroke cancels the stroke.
- [ ] Pencil, brush (all four shapes), eraser, and airbrush keep up with fast strokes at 60 FPS.
- [ ] A second finger mid-stroke removes the stroke and starts a pinch or scroll instead.
- [ ] Two-finger tap undoes; three-finger tap redoes.
- [ ] Zoomed in to 8× or more, pixels look sharp, not blurry.
- [ ] Fill floods a large closed area in well under a second.
- [ ] Select and Free-Form Select: move, resize from the blue corner, flip, rotate, cut, copy, paste.
- [ ] Paste an image copied from Safari or Photos.
- [ ] Text: the keyboard doesn't cover the text box; committed text matches what was typed.
- [ ] Curve bends twice; Polygon closes by tapping the first corner.
- [ ] Edit Colors: drag the spectrum and luminosity bar; OK replaces the last palette color picked.
- [ ] Save to Photos asks for permission the first time, then saves.
- [ ] Open from Photos loads a picture at up to 2048 px.
- [ ] Leave the app, force-quit it, reopen: the picture is still there.
- [ ] VoiceOver reads tool, color, and menu names.
