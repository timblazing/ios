# Court figures

Jack, queen, and king artwork from Dmitry Fomin's English pattern playing cards on Wikimedia Commons (`English pattern <rank> of <suit>.svg`), released under CC0 1.0. The corner indexes, suit marks, and card outline were removed and the art cropped to its 302×482 frame. Colors stay as the source's five flats (`#ff5555`, `#5555aa`, `#ffff55`, black, white); `scripts/make-card-svgs.py` maps them to each set's palette.
