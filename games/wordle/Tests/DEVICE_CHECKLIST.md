# Device checklist (iPad mini 2, iOS 10.3.3)

- [ ] First launch on Wi-Fi shows How to Play, then today's puzzle loads.
- [ ] Typing feels quick, including two-thumb typing; tiles pop.
- [ ] A made-up word shakes the row with "Not in word list"; common words and plurals are accepted.
- [ ] Reveal flips at 60 FPS; keyboard colors update after the flip.
- [ ] Win: praise toast, bounce, then Statistics with the countdown and Share.
- [ ] Share pastes the emoji grid correctly in Notes.
- [ ] Rotate to each orientation: board and keyboard fit, nothing overlaps.
- [ ] Dark Theme and High Contrast recolor everything, including open panels.
- [ ] Hard Mode refuses to turn on mid-round; enforces hints when on.
- [ ] Practice from the menu loads a random past puzzle; New practice puzzle works.
- [ ] With Wi-Fi off: today's puzzle still loads (prefetched); practice falls back to a cached past puzzle or explains.
- [ ] Quit mid-game and relaunch: guesses are restored.
