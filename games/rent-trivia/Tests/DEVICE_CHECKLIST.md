# Device checklist (iPad mini 2)

- [ ] Watch the Movie starts within ~20 s on home Wi-Fi and plays smoothly at 720p.
- [ ] Scrubbing to the middle resumes within a few seconds.
- [ ] Subtitles can be turned on from the player's speech-bubble menu.
- [ ] Done, then Watch the Movie again, offers "Resume from …" at the right spot.
- [ ] Sound plays with the side switch on mute.
- [ ] With Wi-Fi off, tapping Watch the Movie shows "Can't reach TIMTV".
- [ ] Press and hold Watch the Movie opens the API key prompt.
